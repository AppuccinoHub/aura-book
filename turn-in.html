Turn in your recording

  1. Finish your scene and download the file named scena-0N.webm (N is your scene number).
  2. Open the folder for your story below.
  3. Drag the file in, or use New → File upload.
  4. Rename it with your first name, e.g. scena-04-Alex.webm.
